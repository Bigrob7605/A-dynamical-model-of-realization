Phase 1 — operationalizing implicit constructs
Goal: Turn the model's key variables into concrete, empirically measurable constructs before simulation or data collection begins.
D — Cognitive restructuring depth
Measure by pre/post semantic network change. Use free association or concept-mapping tasks around a target problem, then compute real topology deltas (Q, C, L) from the resulting network. This maps the model's abstract restructuring depth onto established computational psycholinguistic methods.
Z — Self-boundary dissolution
Use a state-level adaptation of validated instruments. Combine items from MEQ-30 and the Hartmann Boundary Questionnaire to cover the midrange from ordinary insight to peak states. The goal is a brief state measure that can capture transient boundary shifts during insight episodes.
D, S, A — behavioral operationalization
D (dimensionality): measure through alternative uses or hypothesis generation tasks under uncertainty.
S (stability): measure via short-interval retest on ambiguous interpretation tasks.
A (anchoring/constraint): measure via counterexample tolerance and belief revision tasks.
R(t) — compute cost / resistance
Operationalize with dual-task paradigms during insight-like tasks. Combine cognitive load measures, time pressure, and subjective effort ratings to index λ and β. This provides an empirical handle on the model's cost dynamics.

Core state equations
dD/dt: depth change from anomaly-driven restructuring balanced by resistance and integration fatigue.
dD/dt = α₁·A_in·(1−D) + α₂·Z − α₃·R(t) dS/dt: stability update via support and constraint, weakened by stress and unintegrated change, with low-support erosion gated by a sharp support threshold.
dS/dt = γ₁·σ(Support; k, x₀)·(1−S) − γ₂·Stress·(1−A)·S − 0.04·S·(1−σ(Support; k, x₀)) dA/dt: anchoring increase from evidence and reality feedback, reduced by ambiguity and conflict.
dA/dt = δ₁·Evidence·(1−A) − δ₂·Ambiguity·A dZ/dt: boundary dissolution driven by affect and synthesis, opposed by constraint and re-stabilization.
dZ/dt = ε₁·Emotion·A − ε₂·Constraint·Z Resistance: a time-varying cost term combining working memory load, metabolic cost, and prediction stability.
R(t) = θ_wm·WM(t) + λ·ΔE + β·ΔP
Here A_in is anomaly intensity, Support is social/constraint feedback, and Evidence is the strength of disconfirming or confirming reality checks. The θ and Greek coefficients are heuristic parameters that can be calibrated empirically.

The Biological Compute Window (BCW)
A living neural culture passes through a structured arc: high plasticity → stabilization → degradation. This is formalized as: dL/dt = P(t) · I(t) − δ · L(t) where P(t) = P₀ · e^(−t/τₚ) L(t) = accumulated adaptive capacity · I(t) = information input rate · δ = homeostatic decay · τₚ = plasticity time constant (weeks–months for CL1 in-vitro cultures)

Closed-form solution: L(t) = (P₀·I₀·τₚ / (1 − δτₚ)) · (e^(−δt) − e^(−t/τₚ)) Peak compute time (dL/dt = 0): t* = τₚ · ln(P₀τₚ/δ) / (1/τₚ − δ) Biological Compute Window (total useful adaptive capacity): BCW = ∫₀ᵀ L(t) dt Not FLOPS. Not tokens. Not uptime. The area under the learning curve — before the system returns to silence. For the CL1 (T ≈ 6 months), the peak learning window occurs in the first 1–2 weeks, consistent with reported Doom-navigation training over a matter of days.

D/S/A coordinates of living neural compute
D_B(t) — Dimensionality
H(ρ_B(t)) = −Σ pₖ log pₖ
Shannon entropy of spike-routing across synaptic pathways. High D early (max accessible configurations), decreasing as learning commits the network to stable attractors.
S_B(t) — Stability
1 − P(t)/P₀ = 1 − e^(−t/τₚ)
Complement of plasticity rate. S_B → 0 at birth (maximum revisability), S_B → 1 as the culture stabilizes into committed attractors. Biological analog of commitment accumulation.
A_B(t) — Anchoring
∫₀ᵗ P(τ)·I(τ) dτ = P₀I₀τₚ(1−e^(−t/τₚ))
Accumulated Hebbian learning: the sum of plasticity-weighted stimulation over the culture's experience. Commitment gate fires when A_B(t) > θ_bio — marking critical-period closure.
D/S/A arc of a living culture: t≈0: D high, S low, A low — maximum option breadth (DRAFT phase). 0 < t < t*: D decreases as attractors form, S rises, A accumulates (PROVISIONAL). t≈t*: gate fires (COMMITTED). t > t*: D collapses, S→1 rigid, A plateaus — degradation begins, Ω_B(t)→0.
Coverage-Routing-Gating instantiation in biological compute
Coverage
26 discrete synapse size categories (Salk Institute 2016/2024) encode 4.7 bits/synapse across qualitatively distinct plasticity regimes. 6 biological coverage channels: fast AMPA adaptation (ms–min), STP (s–min), LTP/LTD (min–hrs), structural consolidation (hrs–months), homeostatic scaling (hrs–days), critical-period gate (developmental; irreversible). Each channel handles a failure mode no other substitutes for.
Routing
Hebbian plasticity is the biological routing policy (ρ_B): "neurons that fire together wire together" selects the synaptic pathway matched to the current stimulation regime. Under L2-regularized learning rules, the learning signal can align with Hebbian learning near convergence (Koplow et al., 2025). The routing mechanism was not designed — it evolved.
Gating
Critical periods are the biological commitment gate. DRAFT: silent synapses (AMPA-silent). PROVISIONAL: activity-driven AMPA insertion, LTP accumulating. COMMITTED: PV interneuron maturation, perineuronal net formation, NR2B→NR2A switch. Gate fires when A_B(t) > θ_bio. Rollback requires chondroitinase treatment (high cost).
The two-level hierarchy — mortality as coverage maintenance: At the single-culture level (intra-BCW), CRG operates normally. At the system level (inter-BCW), individual cultures die and are replaced. Death is not a failure mode to engineer around. A committed post-critical-period culture has P(t)≈0 and cannot route to novel regime families. Replacement resets: C_B(0+) = C_max, P(0+) = P₀, A_B(0+) = 0 — full DRAFT state restored. The system maintains Ω_sys(t) > Ω_min across an indefinite horizon precisely by allowing individual cultures to die. We have spent 75 years building computers that try not to die. The VRP may be telling us something about why that was always going to be insufficient.
Formalized mechanisms
Why formalization matters: The model is not just poetic—it has actual structure. Below are the operational definitions of three frequently questioned mechanisms.

Measurement domains: The model operates across three loosely coupled layers—Information (compression, topology), Cognitive (WM, prediction error), Phenomenological (emotion, self-boundary). Mappings between layers are approximate and not metrically equivalent.

① Compute Cost / Resistance

Definition: R(t) = WM_saturation(t) + λ·ΔE_metabolic + β·ΔP_stability

Components:
• WM_saturation: Working memory load. New model must fit within ~3–4 slots of active attention. Saturated WM → cost spike to 1.0; clean fit → near 0.
• ΔE_metabolic: Free-energy change. Old model requires E₀ cal/min; new model costs E₁. If E₁ > E₀, resistance increases proportionally (λ ≈ 0.003 cal⁻¹).
• ΔP_stability: Prediction stability penalty. Each model switch disrupts ongoing predictions. High-stakes contexts (driving, social interaction) → β ≈ 0.8; low-stakes → β ≈ 0.1.

Operationally: If R(t) > θ_reject (typically ~0.6–0.7), the insight is classified as noise and discarded, no matter how valid it is. This is why you don't have a mystical experience every time you find your car keys.

These thresholds are illustrative heuristic ranges derived from cognitive science literature. They are not empirically calibrated constants.

② Viability Filter

Definition: A new model is viable if it maximizes (Compression, Accuracy, Actionability) subject to constraint resolution.

Criteria:
• C (Compression): Kolmogorov complexity. New model must explain more with less code/structure. ΔC > θ_compress (typically ~0.1–0.2 bits/element) required.
• A (Accuracy): Prediction error on held-out reality. Must reduce MAE by ≥ θ_accuracy (typically ~5–15%) on external validation data.
• G (Actionability): Does it guide novel behavior that produces real-world rewards? Binary: yes or no.

Conflict resolution (when C↑ but A↓):
Primary rule: Accuracy is the constraint. If the new model improves compression but worsens predictions, revert to the reject path (stage 07).
Tiebreaker rule: When A is identical, Actionability breaks ties. A model that compresses elegantly but produces no new behavior reverts to reject.

Result: Genuine insight ≠ intellectually satisfying delusion. This is what separates paradigm collapse from pathology.

These criteria thresholds (ΔC > 0.15, MAE ≥10%) are heuristic ranges based on information theory and cognitive modeling. They are illustrative, not empirically derived constants.

③ Field-like Topology (Mathematical Definition)

Definition: Field-like structure is characterized by the loss of modularity (Q) and emergence of global synchrony.

Metrics:
• Q (Modularity score): Measure of distinct clustering. Q ∈ [0,1]. Sparse/Clustered: Q ≈ 0.4–0.6. Integrated: Q ≈ 0.1–0.3. Field-like: Q ≈ 0 (loss of clusters).
• Global Sync (C_clustering coefficient): Probability that neighbors of a node are also neighbors. Low C → linear chains. High C → dense local clusters. Field-like has high C at all scales (near 1.0).
• L (Mean path length): Average steps between nodes. Sparse nets: L ≈ log(N). Field-like: L → 1 (every node reachable in one hop).

Phase transition markers: When Q < θ_modularity (typically ~0.1–0.2) AND C > θ_coherence (typically ~0.8–0.9) simultaneously, the network may undergo topological phase transition. Subjective experience: "self and world boundaries collapse" — geometrically, they do.

These metrics are heuristic ranges illustrating one plausible structural regime that may correspond to reported features of mystical experiences. They are not definitive measurements.

D/S/A composite viability score — from the Law of Generative Control
Ascore = ∫ D(t) · S(t) · A(t) dt

D — Effective Dimensionality: Number of meaningfully accessible hypotheses or action branches. High D = parallel hypothesis maintenance. Low D = narrowed, rigid focus.
S — Temporal Stability: Persistence of active interpretations under perturbation. High S = resistance to volatility. Low S = rapid but unstable updating.
A — Constraint Anchoring: Weight of external verification vs. internal priors. High A = sensory correction dominates. Low A = internal coherence dominates (delusion risk).

For the realization loop: States are not points — they are trajectories (Dt, St, At). Failure corresponds to geometric collapse along one or more axes. Mystical states = high integral value with A → 0 (constraint anchoring temporarily suspended). Pathology = low integral with A remaining low.
Individual differences — why the same anomaly produces different outcomes
The model is a system description, not a destiny. Two people encountering an identical anomaly can end up at opposite poles of the outcome spectrum. The variables below explain why. They are not fixed — each is a target for cultivation.
Prior loop count
Raised sensitivity floor
Each completed loop reshapes the attractor landscape. Practitioners notice smaller anomalies, process them faster, and reach deeper outcomes at lower cost. Prior loop count is the closest proxy for what traditions call "wisdom."
Baseline constraint strength
Reality anchor
A (constraint anchoring) varies by trait. High epistemic humility, empirical orientation, and tolerance of disconfirmation = high baseline A. Low baseline A → even modest anomalies risk pathology at high emotional intensity.
Cognitive flexibility
D — effective dimensionality
High trait flexibility = high D. The system can hold competing hypotheses simultaneously without collapsing to premature commitment. Low flexibility → Viability Filter defaults to the first viable model, not the best one.
Social support density
Re-coherence buffer
SPRT evidence: support (S) acts as a categorical switch near the critical zone. Below S≈0.50 collapse is nearly universal. Above S≈0.75 recovery is nearly guaranteed. Small increases near the boundary produce dramatic outcome shifts — the steepest leverage point in the system.
Induction conditions — what practitioners can actually do
Amplify the anomaly
Deliberately stay with the tension instead of resolving it cheaply. Journaling, dialogue, and contemplative practice all function as anomaly amplifiers — they prevent premature local patching (R < 0.65 escape).
Lower restructuring cost
Reduce WM load and metabolic stakes before entering destabilization. Rest, protected time, low-stakes context (β ≈ 0.1) all reduce R(t) — letting genuine insight through the gate without requiring overwhelming force.
Strengthen re-coherence
Secure integration contexts (trusted people, stable routines, integration practices like writing or teaching) raise S above the critical threshold. The insight is only as durable as the re-coherence environment it lands in.

What would break the model — explicit falsification schedule
This section is part of the model's identity. A model without falsification conditions is not a model — it is a framing device. The criteria below are pre-specified and binding. If any is met, the corresponding component requires revision before re-use.
P1 falsified if
The D → Z → S sequence appears in fewer than 40% of Profound-or-higher trajectories. Current direct test: 68.9% — well above falsification threshold.
P2 falsified if
Support linear fit R² exceeds 0.85. Current R² = 0.3195 — safely in the nonlinear zone. Would indicate support → recovery relationship is effectively linear, negating the threshold structure.
P3 falsified if
The intermediate bin exceeds either the decline or recovery polar cluster. Current intermediate ≈ 10.0% — now consistent with the SPRT borderline target while preserving bimodality. Bimodality criterion passes; current calibration now supports the intermediate bin.
P4 falsified if
Crisis cohort pathology is not ≥5pp higher AND recovery not ≥5pp lower than controls. Current: +5.0pp / −5.1pp — both arms pass. Would indicate crisis initialization does not reliably shift outcome distribution.
Global structure requires revision if
JSD exceeds 0.15. Current JSD = 0.0646 — below trigger. If JSD crosses 0.15 under any reparameterization intended as the canonical model, the SPRT-target connection fails and requires re-examination.
BCW/VRP extension falsified if
Biological degradation trajectories do not align with human/computational D/S/A degradation signatures under harmonized measurement conditions in the cross-substrate test. This test is the decisive arbiter of the third-substrate claim.
